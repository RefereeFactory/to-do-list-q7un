    How it works

    Type a task, choose a priority and an optional due date, then press Add task. Tap the circle to mark something done, use the chips to filter your list, or search to find a task quickly. Overdue dates are flagged gently so nothing slips by.

    Every change is saved to Daylist's secure cloud storage automatically — the small dot near the top shows when it's saving and when it's safely stored. Close the page and come back whenever you like; your list will be right where you left it. There are no accounts and no cards — just you and your day.
